Buffs:

    Barrier: A temporary shield that absorbs a set amount of incoming damage before breaking. 
    Fire Rate Buff: A temporary buff that increases the weapon's rate of fire. 
    Health Regeneration: A temporary buff that restores health over time (HoT). This does not include instant heals or lifesteal. 
    Invincibility: The user cannot be damaged or targeted by hostile effects for a short duration. 
    Lifesteal: A temporary buff that causes the user to heal for a percentage of the damage they deal. 
    Movement Speed Buff: A temporary increase to movement speed. 
    Resistance Buff: A temporary buff that reduces incoming damage from a specific source. 
    Spirit Power Buff: A temporary increase to Spirit Power. 
    Stealth: A temporary buff that renders the user invisible. 
    Hidden on minimap: A temporary buff that renders the user unable to be seen on the minimap. 
    Unstoppable: A powerful buff that grants immunity to crowd control effects like Stun, Silence, Sleep, and Immobilize. The user can still take damage while Unstoppable. 
    Weapon Damage Buff: A temporary increase to weapon damage output.

Debuffs

    Bleed : A negative status effect that deals damage equal to a percentage of a unit's health over time. 
    Burn : A negative status effect that deals Spirit Damage over time. 
    Curse: A negative status effect that interrupts, disarms, and silences abilities and active items. 
    Damage Output Reduction : A negative status effect that reduces the damage output of the afflicted target. Some sources can reduce all damage types outputted, and others may only reduce a specific type. 
    Disarm: A negative status effect that prevents the afflicted target from firing their weapon. They are still capable of casting abilities and using melee attacks. 
    Displace: A negative status effect that changes the direction and acceleration of the target's movement. 
    Healing Reduction: A negative status effect that reduces the target's healing from all sources. 
    Immobilized: A negative status effect that prevents the target from moving. While rooted in place, the target is free to use abilities and fire their weapon. 
    Movement Silence: A negative status effect that applies Movement Slow and prevents the target from casting movement-related abilities and active items, and in some cases further restricts movement. See Slowing Hex for a list of abilities and items affected. 
    Movement Slow: A negative status effect that reduces the movement speed of the target, including sprint speed. Its effectiveness can be reduced through the use of items such as Enduring Speed. 
    Silence : A negative status effect that prevents the target from casting abilities, but they can still use active items (with the exception of sources of curse). It does not disable passive abilities. 
    Sleep: A negative status effect that prevents the target from taking actions, however the target will wake up early should they take any damage. While sleeping, targets can move but extremely slowly. 
    Stun: A negative status effect that completely immobilizes the target and prevents them from taking any actions until the effect expires. 
    Time Stop: A negative status effect that slows the speed of the player's actions, including the effects of inertia and gravity. Also known as Time Slow or Slow Motion. 

Hero Specific Effects

    Affliction: A negative status effect applied by Pocket's Affliction ability. It deals constant damage over time for a long duration, however it is non-lethal and cannot reduce a target's health below 1. It also does not apply item procs. 
    Eternal Night: A negative status effect applied by Drifter's ability of the same name. Inflicted heroes have their vision severely limited. 
    Fixation: A negative status effect that increases the target's damage received from Haze. It is unique to her, applied through her ability of the same name. 
    Hotel Guest: A negative status effect applied by The Doorman's ability of the same name. The target is sent to the Baroness Hotel and has to make their way to the elevator to escape. 


    Types of damage:
        Spirit Damage: Spirit Damage is a form of damage dealt by some abilities and items. It is modifiable by obtaining Spirit Power, and scales with the respective ability or item's Spirit Scaling. Certain items will deal additional Spirit Damage if a condition is met.
